2. General information

Protecting your personal data is important to us. Our iOS app is designed so that personal data is generally not transmitted to us or to third parties.

The app may store content and settings exclusively on your device so that its functions can be used.

3. Data processing in the app

Our app processes data exclusively on the user's device.

In particular, this means:

  • no transfer of personal data to us,
  • no transfer to third parties,
  • no user tracking,
  • no analytics or advertising services,
  • no server-side storage of app content,
  • no creation of user profiles.

4. No data collection by us

We do not receive personal data through the app and cannot access content that users store or enter in the app, provided that it is stored only locally on the device.

5. Permissions

The app does not access sensitive device features such as contacts, location, microphone, camera or photos unless this is explicitly described in the app and technically necessary.

If individual device features are used, this is solely to provide the relevant app function and generally takes place locally on the device.

6. Legal basis

To the extent that personal data is processed on the user's device at all, this happens only insofar as it is technically necessary to use the app.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where processing is necessary to use the app, or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR based on our legitimate interest in providing an app that functions correctly.

7. Storage period

Content and settings stored in the app generally remain on the user's device until the user deletes them or uninstalls the app.

Because no data is transmitted to our systems, we cannot view or delete locally stored data.

8. Recipients of data

No personal data from the app is transmitted to us or to third parties.

9. Rights of data subjects

Where personal data is processed, data subjects generally have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • right of access,
  • right to rectification,
  • right to erasure,
  • right to restriction of processing,
  • right to data portability,
  • right to object,
  • right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Please note: Because we do not receive or store personal data from the app, we generally cannot identify a particular person from it.

10. Hosting of this website

When you visit this website, the hosting provider may process server log data needed for technical operation, in particular:

  • IP address,
  • date and time of access,
  • browser type and version,
  • operating system,
  • referrer URL,
  • pages accessed.

This processing serves the technical delivery, security and stability of the website.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Further information can be found in the hosting provider's privacy policy: Hosting provider link

For privacy questions, contact us at: privacy [at] schubertmarkus [dot] de
